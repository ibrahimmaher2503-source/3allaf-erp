# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TOY & JOY serves System Administrators, Branch Managers, Cashiers, Purchasing Officers, Warehouse Managers, Pricing Officers, Party Managers, Stock Counters, and Accountants/Reviewers. They work on desktop and tablet devices in stores, warehouses, offices, and cashier stations, often under time pressure and with mixed technical experience. Their primary jobs are configuring operations, moving and counting stock, completing fast sales, reconciling shifts, serving customers, and coordinating party services and rental assets.

## Product Purpose

Provide one trustworthy Arabic-first retail and party operations system that is fast enough for daily branch work and controlled enough for inventory, money, approvals, and audit. Success means users complete the correct workflow with few clicks, understand sensitive actions before committing them, and can trust that approved history, role boundaries, stock, payments, and separated Product and Party Wallets remain accurate.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and energetic. The product should feel capable and approachable to non-technical operators without becoming playful at the expense of operational seriousness.

## Anti-references

- Decorative SaaS dashboards that prioritize visual effects over operational clarity.
- Heavy admin themes with deep nested navigation, slow tables, or generic CRUD screens for POS.
- Dark interfaces chosen by category reflex rather than the bright store and office environment.
- Glass effects, gradient text, ornamental animation, oversized metric templates, nested card grids, and non-standard controls.
- Interfaces that hide authorization or validation failures, mix retail and party activity, or expose cross-wallet financial data.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the current task and its next safe action obvious.
2. Use familiar, consistent controls so the interface disappears into the work.
3. Make sensitive state, scope, totals, approvals, and exceptions explicit before commitment.
4. Optimize cashier and warehouse paths for keyboard, scanner, touch, and minimal clicks.
5. Treat Arabic RTL, English LTR, accessibility, loading, empty, error, success, and denied states as core product behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, complete labels and validation associations, visible keyboard focus, comfortable touch targets, readable contrast, status cues that do not rely on color alone, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts for approved desktop and tablet sizes. Arabic is the primary operational direction with correct RTL behavior; English LTR is fully supported. Typography and copy must remain readable for non-technical users and dense operational data.
